Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

This notice explains what personal data FlatChat processes when you use the assistant, why, and the choices you have. FlatChat helps you search for apartments in Berlin through conversation.

Who is responsible

FlatChat is a student research project developed at Freie Universität Berlin. The data controller for the purposes of the GDPR is Nadir Bašić, contactable at basicnadir@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Account data — the email address you sign in with.
  • Your conversations — the messages you send and the assistant's replies, kept so you can reload and continue past chats.
  • Search context — the filters and results derived from your messages, including any location you tell us (for example, where you live or work) when you ask to search near a place.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use this data solely to provide the service you asked for: to run your apartment search — including searching near a location you give us — and to keep a usable history of your conversations. Our legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): the assistant cannot carry out a location-aware search or restore your history without processing this data. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Apartment listings

The apartment listings we show are collected from public rental sites. We deliberately do not store the personal details of whoever posted a listing — names, phone numbers, email addresses and profiles are removed before the data is saved. We keep only the property's own details (location, size, rent, images, description with contact details redacted).

Who we share it with

To generate replies, your messages are processed by our AI model provider acting as our processor. In production this runs on an EU-resident deployment (Azure OpenAI, EU Data Zone), so processing and storage stay within the European Union, and your inputs are not used to train models. We also use hosting infrastructure to run the service. We do not share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, conversations and search context for as long as your account is active, so your history stays available. You can delete any conversation at any time, and deletion is immediate and permanent.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it erased — you can delete a conversation directly in the app (this permanently removes its messages and search context), and you may contact us to erase your account;
  • rectify inaccurate data and object to processing;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for Berlin, the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (datenschutz-berlin.de).

To exercise any of these rights, contact basicnadir@gmail.com.
