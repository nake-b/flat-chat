What's already out there

Tools exist. They optimize speed, not fit.

Bucket 1 · Listing platforms

Fast filters, no judgement

ImmoScout24 · WG-Gesucht · Kleinanzeigen · Immowelt
Checkbox filters that can't combine soft, human criteria.
Bucket 2 · Notification bots

Race-to-apply speed

flathunter · WohnungsBot · Homeboy · immonotify
Help you apply first — never help you decide if it fits.
Bucket 3 · AI search experiments

Thin & elsewhere

SearchPlace.ai — Vienna-only
Closest in spirit, but dated UX and not built for Berlin.

None use modern LLM reasoning. None feel good to use. Fragmentation is itself the problem.

The unsolved gap

Speed is solved. Fit isn't.Three groups every existing tool leaves behind:

Long-term home seekers
Planning to stay 5+ years. Getting it right matters far more than applying first.
Picky, specific needs
Quiet, near a park, balcony, ground-floor for the dog — filters can't combine these, let alone judge them.
Hassle-averse users
Tired of juggling sites and copy-paste templates. Want to describe it and have someone narrow it down.
Our angle Flat Chat is the AI version of a great real-estate agent — always available, knows the city, judges fit.
