What to say for each point

01 · ScrapingIngestion
Modern listing sites ship real anti-bot protection — it took real effort to get through. We manage it, but a reliable 24/7 pipeline against defenses that keep changing would be its own challenge. And we did it responsibly: within each site's Terms of Service & robots.txt, minimal requests, polite crawl rates.
02 · Data engineeringArchitecture
We push the expensive work upstream. A pre-aggregating gold layer computes the heavy joins and geo/semantic enrichment once, inside the ETL. Search then reads precomputed rows — so user queries stay fast while the slow work runs offline in the pipeline, not on the request path.
03 · UsersProduct
However proud we were of the build, the first user tests revealed how it actually behaves in the real world. They surfaced UX issues we'd never have caught ourselves and drove concrete adjustments — real users, not internal confidence, are the ground truth.
